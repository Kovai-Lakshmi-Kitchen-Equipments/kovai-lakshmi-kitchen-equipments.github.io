Tray Type Pulverizer

Technical Specifications

Motor Power 2 HP, 3 HP and 5 HP
Capacity 30 - 80 kg per hour, depending on model and material
Power Supply Single phase for 2 HP and 3 HP; three phase for 5 HP
Grinding Mechanism Mesh-based grinding system
Material Of Construction MS body with SS food-contact parts
Output Fineness Variable mesh sizes
Suitable For Chilli, turmeric, coriander, spices, grains, pulses and other suitable dry materials

Product Information

Our Tray Type Pulverizer is designed for efficient grinding of spices, grains, pulses and other dry ingredients. Available in 2 HP, 3 HP and 5 HP motor options, it offers a capacity ranging from 30 kg to 80 kg per hour, depending on the model and material being processed. The machine features a mild steel (MS) body with stainless steel (SS) food-contact parts for hygienic processing. Its interchangeable mesh sizes help achieve the desired powder fineness, making it suitable for commercial food processing units, spice manufacturers and grinding businesses.

How To Use

  1. Place the machine on a level, stable surface and ensure proper electrical earthing.
  2. Select and fit the appropriate mesh size for the required powder fineness.
  3. Check that all components, covers and safety guards are properly secured.
  4. Connect the machine to the correct power supply for the selected motor.
  5. Switch on the machine according to the operating instructions.
  6. Feed clean, dry material gradually without overloading the machine.
  7. Collect the processed powder from the designated outlet or tray.
  8. Switch off and isolate the power supply before cleaning.

Maintenance

  • Switch off and isolate the power supply before cleaning or servicing.
  • Clean the mesh, grinding chamber, tray and SS food-contact surfaces after use.
  • Inspect the mesh regularly for wear, blockage or damage.
  • Check the motor, bearings, belts and fasteners periodically, where applicable.
  • Keep the motor and electrical components free from dust and moisture.
  • Replace worn or damaged components as required.
  • Arrange periodic servicing through a qualified technician.

Caution

  • Never insert hands or tools into the grinding area while the machine is running.
  • Always isolate the power supply before cleaning, opening or servicing.
  • Use only clean, dry materials suitable for the machine.
  • Do not overload the machine during operation.
  • Ensure all covers and safety guards are secured before starting.
  • Use the correct phase and electrical supply for the selected motor.
  • Wear suitable protective equipment when handling chilli and other irritating powders.
  • Allow only trained personnel to operate the machine.
