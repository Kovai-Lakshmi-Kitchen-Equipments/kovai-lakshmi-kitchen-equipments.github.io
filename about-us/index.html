About Us

A family business making strong, reliable grinders and food-processing machines in Coimbatore since 1983.

Welcome To Kovai Lakshmi Grinders

Coimbatore (Kovai) is known across India as the home of the wet grinder and Kovai Lakshmi Grinders is proud to be part of that tradition. Since 1983, we have been making strong, reliable grinders that help families and businesses prepare soft idlis, crispy dosas and fresh chutneys every day.

Every grinder we build combines traditional stone-grinding methods with modern engineering. The result is a machine that keeps the authentic taste of home-made batter and makes daily cooking easier.

A Family Business Since 1983

Kovai Lakshmi Grinders was founded in Coimbatore in 1983 with a clear vision: to give kitchens and food businesses machinery they can rely on every day. Over four decades the company has grown steadily and it is still run by the same family today.

Today the company is in its third generation of family leadership, represented by Danusree Rajendran, Chief Operating Officer. The family carries forward the same commitment to quality, honest advice and customer satisfaction that the company was built on.

Our Factory And Team

We build our machines in our own factory at Thanneer Panthal Road, Peelamedu, Coimbatore - 641004. A dedicated 12-member factory team takes care of manufacturing and product quality, supported by our management, sales representatives, supervisors, store assistants and other team members.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring quality, durable and affordable grinders to every kitchen. We aim to:

  • Build grinders that last for years with minimal maintenance
  • Keep the natural taste and texture of traditionally ground batter
  • Offer fair prices without compromising on quality
  • Support our customers before, during and long after their purchase

Our Vision

We want Kovai Lakshmi Grinders to be the most trusted grinder brand in homes and commercial kitchens across India and beyond. We see a future where every family enjoys healthy, home-made food prepared with ease and where the name "Kovai Lakshmi" stands for quality worldwide.

Customer Service

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. When you choose Kovai Lakshmi Grinders, you get more than a product. You get a long-term relationship.

  • Expert Guidance: We explain capacity, applications and specifications clearly, so you choose the machine that suits your production needs.
  • Installation And Demonstration: We help install machines at your location and show your team how to operate them.
  • Delivery Across India: We supply machinery to customers across the country, with clear communication and timely delivery.
  • Warranty Support: All our grinders come with a 1 - 2 year warranty for your peace of mind.
  • Quick After-Sales Service: Our trained technicians respond quickly to service requests.
  • Genuine Spare Parts: Original parts are always available to keep your grinder running like new.
  • Easy To Reach: Contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, or visit our shops in Coimbatore, Chennai and Salem.

Environment Friendly

We care about the planet as much as we care about your kitchen. Sustainability is built into the way we work.

  • Energy-efficient motors that use less electricity and lower your power bills
  • Natural grinding stones that need no chemical coatings and give healthier food
  • Long-lasting design that reduces waste, since a grinder built to last doesn't need frequent replacement
  • Eco-conscious packaging made with recyclable materials wherever possible
  • Responsible manufacturing that minimises waste and energy use in our factory
