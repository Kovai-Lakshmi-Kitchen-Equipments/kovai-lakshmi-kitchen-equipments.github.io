Oil Dryer

Technical Specifications

Capacity 10 liters (approx. 3–4 kg per batch)
Motor 0.5 HP single-phase
Material Food Grade Stainless Steel
Operation Type Semi-Automatic
Output Quick and uniform oil removal in minutes

Product Information

The Oil Dryer (10 Liter) is a compact and efficient machine designed to remove excess oil from fried food items such as chips, murukku, mixture, and namkeen. Made from high-quality food-grade stainless steel, it ensures hygienic operation and long-lasting performance. This model is ideal for small snack manufacturers, hotels, and catering units. The machine operates using centrifugal force to extract oil uniformly, keeping snacks crisp and oil-free.

How To Use

  1. Place the fried food items evenly inside the oil dryer drum.
  2. Close the lid securely.
  3. Switch on the motor — the drum will start spinning at high speed.
  4. Allow the machine to run for 1–2 minutes to remove excess oil.
  5. Switch off the motor and carefully remove the dried snacks.

Caution

  • Do not overload the drum beyond its rated capacity.
  • Ensure the lid is properly closed before starting the machine.
  • Never open the lid while the drum is spinning.
  • Clean the inner drum after every batch to maintain hygiene.
  • Disconnect power before cleaning or maintenance.
