Murukku Making Machine - Advanced Type

Technical Specifications

Model Advanced Type Murukku Making Machine
Material High-grade Stainless Steel (Food-grade)
Motor Power 1 HP to 2 HP (as per requirement)
Capacity 80 - 120 kg per hour (approx.)
Operation Type Fully or Semi-Automatic (based on configuration)
Feeding System Automatic dough feeding with consistent pressure control
Output Type Continuous extrusion with conveyor option
Nozzle Options Multiple interchangeable dies for different murukku varieties
Power Supply Single or Three Phase / 220 - 440V
Design Compact, heavy-duty and easy to operate

Product Information

The Kovai Lakshmi Murukku Making Machine (Advanced Type) is a high-performance, fully automated solution designed for large-scale snack and food manufacturing units. Engineered with precision, this model delivers faster production, uniform quality and superior efficiency compared to the basic model.

It is ideal for commercial kitchens, snack factories, sweet shops and food industries aiming to achieve consistent output with minimal manual effort and maximum hygiene.

Our Advanced Murukku Making Machine is built with cutting-edge technology and robust stainless-steel construction. It comes equipped with an automatic dough feeding system, speed control and interchangeable nozzles to produce a variety of snacks such as murukku, thenkuzhal, chakli, twist, kai murukku and more.

This model ensures uniform thickness, shape and texture while maintaining the authentic taste of traditional South Indian snacks.

How To Use

  1. Prepare the dough with the right texture for murukku.
  2. Load the dough into the automatic feeding chamber.
  3. Choose the required nozzle based on the desired shape and size.
  4. Set the machine speed and start the operation.
  5. The machine extrudes murukku strands directly onto a tray or into the fryer conveyor.
  6. Collect the output, fry and pack for sale.
  7. Clean the machine parts after each batch for hygiene and durability.

Key Benefits

  • High Production Capacity: Ideal for large-scale murukku production.
  • Consistent Quality: Ensures uniform size, thickness and texture in every piece.
  • Automatic Operation: Reduces manual effort and increases productivity.
  • Durable & Hygienic Design: Made from food-grade stainless steel for long life.
  • Customizable Output: Adjustable speed and multiple dies for different product styles.
  • Low Maintenance: Easy to clean, reliable motor and smooth operation.
  • Energy Efficient: Optimized design for power savings.
