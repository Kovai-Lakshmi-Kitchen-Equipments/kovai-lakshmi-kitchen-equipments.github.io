Masala Coating Machine

Technical Specifications

Body Material Stainless Steel (SS 304 Grade)
Drum Type Octagonal / Round (customizable)
Motor Power 0.5 HP – 1 HP
Voltage 220–240 V / Single Phase
Capacity 10 kg to 30 kg per batch (depending on product type)
Rotation Speed Variable with speed control
Heating System (Optional) Electric heater for dry coating
Machine Weight Approx. 60–80 kg
Finish Mirror polish for hygiene and easy cleaning

Product Information

The Masala Coating Machine is designed to evenly coat fried snacks like potato chips, peanuts, sev, and namkeen with spices, seasonings, or flavoring powders. This efficient and hygienic machine ensures uniform mixing and coating, enhancing the taste and appearance of snacks. Made from high-grade stainless steel, it is ideal for snack manufacturers, catering units, and food industries involved in seasoning and flavoring operations.

How To Use

  1. Load the fried or roasted snacks into the coating drum.
  2. Add masala, seasoning, or liquid flavoring as required.
  3. Switch on the machine and adjust the rotation speed.
  4. Allow the machine to rotate until all snacks are evenly coated.
  5. Discharge the finished product through the outlet and pack as needed.

Maintenance

  • Clean the drum thoroughly after each use to prevent flavor residue.
  • Check motor alignment and belt tension periodically.
  • Avoid water entry into electrical components.
  • Lubricate bearings and moving parts as recommended.
  • Store in a dry place when not in use.

Caution

  • Do not overload the drum beyond the rated capacity.
  • Avoid adding wet masala unless the machine supports liquid coating.
  • Ensure the lid is closed securely before operation.
  • Always switch off the power before cleaning or maintenance.

Key Benefits

  • Ensures uniform coating of spices and flavors.
  • Reduces manual mixing and improves production efficiency.
  • Food-grade stainless steel ensures hygiene and easy cleaning.
  • Equipped with variable speed control for different snack types.
  • Compact design suitable for small and large-scale snack units.
  • Durable and low-maintenance construction for long service life.
