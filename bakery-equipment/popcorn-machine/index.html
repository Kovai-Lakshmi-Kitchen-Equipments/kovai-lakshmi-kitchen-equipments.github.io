Popcorn Machine

Technical Specifications

Body Material Stainless Steel with Glass Enclosure
Heating Type Electric heating element
Voltage 220 - 240 V / Single Phase
Power Consumption 1.2 kW - 1.5 kW
Kettle Capacity 250 g - 500 g per batch (approx. 3 - 5 minutes per batch)
Output Capacity 4 - 5 kg/hour
Lighting Internal lamp for visibility and display
Structure Table-top / Counter model
Finish Powder-coated steel frame with transparent glass sides

Product Information

The Popcorn Machine is a compact and efficient appliance designed to produce crispy, delicious popcorn in just a few minutes. Built with a stainless steel body and a high-performance heating system, it ensures fast popping with minimal oil usage. Ideal for theatres, food stalls, restaurants, schools and events, this machine combines attractive design with reliable performance for both commercial and small business use.

How To Use

  1. Plug in and switch on the machine.
  2. Preheat the kettle for 2 - 3 minutes.
  3. Add a measured quantity of oil, corn kernels and seasoning into the kettle.
  4. Close the lid and allow the popcorn to pop.
  5. Once popping slows down, tilt the kettle to release popcorn into the collection tray.
  6. Serve hot or store in airtight containers.

Maintenance

  • Clean the kettle and tray after every use with a soft, damp cloth.
  • Do not immerse the kettle in water; wipe it instead.
  • Remove leftover kernels to avoid clogging.
  • Check electrical cords and switches periodically.
  • Store the machine in a dry, dust-free environment.

Caution

  • Avoid touching the kettle or inner surfaces during or immediately after use - surfaces will be hot.
  • Do not operate the machine without corn or oil.
  • Use only food-grade oil and corn kernels.
  • Keep away from water contact near electrical parts.
  • Switch off the power before cleaning or maintenance.

Key Benefits

  • Produces fresh, crispy popcorn within minutes.
  • Energy-efficient and easy to operate.
  • Hygienic stainless steel and glass construction for durability.
  • Ideal for cinema halls, canteens and events.
  • Equipped with internal lighting for an attractive display.
  • Compact, portable and easy to clean.
