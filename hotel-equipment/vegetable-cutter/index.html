Vegetable Cutter

Technical Specifications

Capacity 100–200 kg/hr (depending on model)
Motor 0.5 HP – 1 HP (single-phase / three-phase)
Material Food Grade Stainless Steel
Operation Type Semi-Automatic / Electric
Output Uniformly cut vegetables with clean, smooth edges
Blades Can be suggested according to the purpose of cutting

Product Information

The Vegetable Cutter is a versatile and high-efficiency machine designed to cut, slice, shred, or dice vegetables and fruits quickly and uniformly. Built with food-grade stainless steel and equipped with multiple cutting blades, it ensures precise and hygienic operation suitable for restaurants, catering units, hotels, and large-scale kitchens. This machine is ideal for preparing ingredients for salads, curries, snacks, and garnishing — saving both time and labor.

How To Use

  1. Wash and prepare the vegetables.
  2. Choose the appropriate cutting blade (slicing, shredding, dicing, or chopping).
  3. Load the vegetables into the feeding chute.
  4. Switch on the motor — the machine will instantly cut the vegetables as per the selected blade.
  5. Collect the cut vegetables from the outlet tray and clean the machine after use.

Caution

  • Do not insert hands or tools into the feeding chute while the machine is running.
  • Always turn off and unplug the machine before changing blades.
  • Do not overload the machine with hard or large uncut vegetables.
  • Clean all parts thoroughly after every use to maintain hygiene.
  • Ensure the machine is placed on a stable and dry surface before operation.

Key Benefits

  • Multi-Functional: Performs slicing, dicing, shredding, and chopping with ease.
  • Time & Labor Saving: Cuts large quantities quickly and efficiently.
  • Uniform Cutting: Ensures consistent size and shape for professional food presentation.
  • Hygienic & Safe: Entirely made of stainless steel for food safety.
  • Easy to Operate: Simple control and quick blade change system.
  • Durable & Reliable: Heavy-duty motor ensures long-lasting performance.
  • Ideal for Commercial Use: Perfect for restaurants, canteens, and food processing units.
