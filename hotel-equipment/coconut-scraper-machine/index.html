Product Information

The Kovai Lakshmi Coconut Scraper Machine is specially designed to make coconut scraping quick, safe and effortless. Built with a sturdy stainless steel body and sharp blades, this machine ensures uniform scraping of coconut meat in seconds. It is ideal for commercial kitchens, hotels, catering units and households where coconuts are used frequently in cooking. Designed for both efficiency and hygiene, it helps save time and reduces manual effort.

How To Use

  1. Place the machine on a flat and stable surface.
  2. Connect to the correct power supply.
  3. Clean the coconut shell and break it into halves.
  4. Hold the coconut firmly and press gently against the rotating blade.
  5. Allow the scraper to remove the white coconut meat evenly.
  6. Once done, switch off the machine and clean the blade area thoroughly.

Maintenance

  • Clean the blade and surrounding area after each use to prevent clogging.
  • Check the blade for sharpness periodically and replace if worn out.
  • Avoid using water directly on the motor.
  • Ensure all parts are dry before reassembling.
  • Keep the machine covered when not in use to prevent dust buildup.

Caution

  • Always switch off the machine before cleaning or changing parts.
  • Keep hands and fingers away from the rotating blade during use.
  • Do not operate with wet hands.
  • Ensure proper earthing and power connection.
  • Keep away from children and untrained users.

Key Benefits

  • Hygienic and time-saving operation.
  • High-quality stainless steel construction for long life.
  • Provides uniform and fine coconut scrapings.
  • Easy to operate, clean and maintain.
  • Compact design suitable for small and large kitchens.
  • Reduces manual labor and ensures faster output.
