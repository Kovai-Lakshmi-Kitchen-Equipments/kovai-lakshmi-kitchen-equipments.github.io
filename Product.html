Chapathi Making Machine

Technical Specifications

Capacity 1000 - 1200 Pieces / Hour
Motor Power 1.5 HP Single / Three Phase
Material High Grade Food Safe Stainless Steel
Thickness Control Adjustable (1mm to 4mm)
Weight Approx. 180 Kg

Product Information

Our Chapathi Making Machine is engineered for high-performance commercial environments, combining heavy-duty durability with precision engineering. Designed specifically for hotels, industrial canteens, and large-scale catering services, this machine ensures that every chapathi is perfectly circular and uniform in texture. By automating the flattening process, it eliminates the inconsistencies of manual rolling while significantly reducing production time and labor costs.


Constructed with food-grade stainless steel and equipped with high-torque motors, this machine is capable of continuous operation even during peak hours. Whether you are running a busy hospital kitchen or a premium bakery, this equipment provides the reliable output needed to scale your business efficiently.

How To Use

Operating this machinery is designed to be user-friendly and efficient, minimizing manual labor in your commercial kitchen.


  • Prepare your dough to the desired consistency.
  • Adjust the roller settings for your required thickness.
  • Feed the dough continuously into the top hopper.
  • Collect the perfectly uniform output at the delivery tray.
  • Clean the rollers and surfaces easily after operations.

Key Benefits

Investing in automated preparation equipment significantly boosts your kitchen's daily output and consistency.


  • High Output: Dramatically increases production speed.
  • Uniformity: Ensures consistent size and quality for every piece.
  • Labor Saving: Reduces dependency on skilled manual labor.
  • Hygienic: Food-grade stainless steel ensures safety and easy cleaning.
  • Durable: Built with heavy-duty components for long-lasting operation.
